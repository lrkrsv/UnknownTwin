# UnknownTwin

# 🎓 UniTwin: Making Unknown University Remotely Accessible

> Built in **6 hours** at the *Securing the Future* hackathon (Cursor Hackathon, The Hague) 🏆

Millions of students never make it to a university lecture hall, not because they lack the drive or the talent, but because tuition, relocation, and the cost of simply being on campus get in the way. UniTwin was our answer to that problem: what if the campus could come to the student instead?

UniTwin is a full virtual campus, not just a video library. It gives students an AI tutor trained on real lecture content, a way to reach a real human teacher when they need one, lectures dubbed into their own language, and a social space where they can hang out, practice pitches, cook, and work out, all from a browser, anywhere in the world.

---

## 💡 The Challenge

We were asked to design a platform for students who cannot afford tuition, relocation, or the everyday cost of attending a physical university. Our goal was to remove the financial and geographic barriers to education without losing what actually makes university valuable: the people, the community, and the sense of belonging to something.

We believe education shouldn't be a privilege tied to a postcode or a bank balance. UniTwin is our small proof that a genuinely global classroom, one that works just as well for a student in Lagos, Lima, or Delft, is possible with the tools available today.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎥 Multilingual Lecture Hub
Lectures are automatically dubbed into multiple languages using the **ElevenLabs API**, so language is never the reason a student gets left behind. We demoed Spanish, French, and Dutch, but the approach scales to any language a student needs.

### 🤖 An AI Tutor That Actually Knows Your Course
Every student gets a personal AI twin of their university. Ask it a question and it answers using the actual lectures and files their teachers uploaded, not generic internet knowledge. Say "quiz me" and it builds a quiz on the spot from that same material.

### 🙋 A Real Human Is Always One Message Away
We built UniTwin around a simple belief: AI should support human connection, not replace it. Students can drop a request into the Teacher Basket with their name, email, and topic. Teachers see it land in their own hub and reply directly, so no student is stuck talking to a machine when they need a person.

### 🧑‍🏫 Teacher Hub
Teachers can upload, publish, and delete lectures, attach supporting files, and manage every incoming student request from a single dashboard, so running a remote course feels as easy as running an in-person one.

### 🌆 A Living Virtual Campus
Students build a customizable avatar and pick a learner type, so the experience adapts to how they actually learn. From the campus, they can:
- **Join live classrooms**, chat with classmates, and take part in real time, wherever they are in the world
- **Record a pitch and get AI feedback**, with an AI coach reviewing structure (problem, solution, traction) and pacing (aim for 60 seconds)
- **Go solo** and drop straight into the AI tutor
- **Visit the kitchen** for AI-generated recipes, like an evening golden milk
- **Hit the gym**, with a fresh AI-generated workout every day

## 🌍 Why It Matters
Access to education is still one of the clearest dividing lines in the world. UniTwin is a hackathon-sized attempt at closing that gap: a campus that doesn't care where you live, what you earn, or what language you speak, but that still gives you a teacher who answers, classmates who show up, and a place that feels like yours. We built it in six hours because we wanted to prove it's possible. We'd love to build the version that actually changes someone's life.
