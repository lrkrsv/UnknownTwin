# 🎓 UniTwin — An AI Campus for Students Who Can't Afford to Be There

> Built in **6 hours** at the *Securing the Future* hackathon (Cursor Hackathon, The Hague) 🏆

UniTwin reimagines higher education for students who are priced out of attending university in person. It's not just an e-learning portal — it's a full **virtual campus**, complete with an AI tutor trained on real lecture content, live human mentorship, multilingual dubbing, and a social space where students can hang out, practice pitches, cook, and work out, all without leaving the browser.

---

## 💡 The Challenge

We were asked to design a platform for students who cannot afford tuition, relocation, or the general cost of attending a physical university. Our answer: strip away the financial and geographic barriers while keeping the two things that make university *feel* like university — human connection and community — intact.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎥 Multilingual Lecture Hub
Lectures are automatically dubbed into multiple languages using the **ElevenLabs API**, so students can learn in the language they're most comfortable in (we demoed Spanish, French, and Dutch).

### 🤖 AI Tutor, Trained on Your Actual Lectures
Every student gets a personal AI twin of their university — ask it questions, and it pulls answers directly from the lectures and files their teachers upload. Say `"quiz me"` and it generates a quiz on the spot from that material.

### 🙋 Ask a Human Mentor
AI is a tool, not a replacement for people. Students can drop a request into the **Teacher Basket** with their name, email, and topic. Teachers see incoming requests in their own hub, respond directly, and the conversation flows back to the student — keeping a real human in the loop whenever it matters.

### 🧑‍🏫 Teacher Hub
Teachers can upload, publish, and delete lectures, drop in supporting files, and manage all incoming student requests from one dashboard.

### 🌆 Virtual Campus
A fully customizable avatar and learner-type profile personalize the experience to how each student actually learns. From the campus, students can:
- **Join live classrooms** — chat with classmates, hop into a session, and interact in real time
- **Record a pitch and get AI feedback** — an AI coach reviews structure (problem, solution, traction), timing (aim for 60 seconds), and delivery
- **Go solo** — jump straight into the AI tutor
- **Visit the kitchen** — get AI-generated recipes (evening golden milk, anyone?)
- **Hit the gym** — a fresh, AI-generated workout accordion, updated daily
